## 1. What this app is

Book Scanner is a Progressive Web App that photographs a book cover, reads the title
and author using on-device OCR, and lets you keep a personal library of what you own
or have read. It has no account system, no server, and no cloud database — your
photos and data stay on your own device unless you export them yourself.

It is provided **as a free hobby project, with no guarantee of support, updates, or
continued availability.**
